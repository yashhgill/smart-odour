Why this exists

The problem

A chicken farm sits beside the UTeM campus at Durian Tunggal. Its smell drifts over the campus, mostly at night, and until now there was no record of when it happens, how often or where it is worst.

What we built

A small sensor station measures the air every minute and sends it here. It learns what clean air looks like at its location, then shows how far the air has moved from that as a simple number. If the smell lasts, everyone who signed up gets a Telegram alert.

Where it covers

The first sensor is assigned to Kolej Kediaman Lekiu (Satria). The system is ready for more sensors at Kolej Kediaman Lestari, the main cafeteria, FTMK and the farm side, each shown as its own point on the map.

What the station uses

Four MQ gas sensors, a DHT temperature and humidity sensor, a small fan that draws air across them and an LCD screen, all run by an ESP32.

What comes next

The MQ sensors detect gases, not dust. Adding a particle sensor such as the PMS5003 would let the same station and dashboard also track haze.

Help it get more accurate

When you smell something, or when you don't, use "Can you smell it now?" at the top. Your answers show what the numbers mean in real life, and they are used to set the Noticeable and Strong levels.
